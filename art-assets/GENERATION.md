# Architectural texture sources

These three textures were created with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. They are illustrative architectural interpretations, not photographs or surveys of individual London buildings. The PNG originals are retained here; the app serves JPEG copies from `dist/assets/`. No API key is required at runtime.

| --- | --- | --- |
| `art-assets/portland-facade-v1.png` | `dist/assets/portland-facade-v1.jpg` | Repeated Portland stone office façades |

| `art-assets/westminster-facade-v1.png` | `dist/assets/westminster-facade-v1.jpg` | Gothic detail on the Palace's 3D walls |

## Portland stone prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless architectural PBR base color texture for actual 3D buildings in an interactive London world. Create a square extremely detailed, photographic, perfectly front-on ORTHOGRAPHIC wall elevation of an elegant weathered late Victorian London Portland limestone office building. The entire image is exactly a repeating grid of FOUR equal width window bays by FOUR equal height storeys, 4x4 windows, all identical grid spacing. Rectangular vertical sash windows with dark grey frames, delicate mullions, some partially closed curtains and dark realistic reflections, fine lintel and sill carvings, very subtle soot below sills, natural patina, stone joints, architectural mouldings. Flat overcast neutral lighting, NO cast shadows except tiny contact in recesses. No perspective at all. The whole elevation is one repeatable mid-building section: NO ground level, no entrances, NO roof, NO sky, NO people, NO street, NO borders, no surrounding scene, NO text. Edge-to-edge wall. The left and right edges join seamlessly and top and bottom edges join seamlessly in a repeated texture. Each window module is 3 metres wide and 3.6 metres tall. Credible high-resolution photographed London architecture with real surface imperfections, restrained warm grey-beige stone and near-black glazing. Do not render a miniature or illustration. This will be tiled on 3D wall meshes.

## Stock brick prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless base color facade texture to wrap on real 3D London buildings. Make an extremely detailed photographic, completely front-on ORTHOGRAPHIC elevation of a weathered London yellow-brown stock brick Victorian terrace wall. Exact repeating FOUR window bays across by FOUR storeys high grid, 4x4 windows, evenly spaced white-painted wood sash windows, limestone lintels and sills, dark recessed glass, some lace curtains, subtly different interior darkness. Muted yellow ochre and soot-darkened brown bricks with authentic fine mortar and chipped edges, little signs of age. Neutral diffuse overcast light, no large cast shadows, just contact shadows in the recesses. Edge-to-edge wall texture, the top and bottom as well as left and right edges MUST tile seamlessly. No perspective, no sky, no roof, no ground floor, no door, no people, no street, no surrounding scene, no letters, no borders. This image is only a mid-building section with a repetitive window grid, each bay 3 metres wide, each storey 3.6 metres tall. Materials must resemble high resolution real architectural photography, absolutely not illustration or a 3D toy.

## Westminster prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless flat architectural facade texture for the Palace of Westminster riverside wall in an interactive 3D London scene. A perfectly front-on ORTHOGRAPHIC high resolution photograph-like elevation of a repeating middle section of the Palace of Westminster's intricate honey-grey Anston limestone Gothic Revival facade. Exactly FOUR tall narrow pointed Gothic window bays across and TWO storeys high, 4 columns x 2 rows. Deep dark recessed glazed lancet windows with delicate stone tracery, carved lintels, slender pilasters between bays, ornate interlaced stone panels below windows, horizontal string courses. Fine natural stone detail, realistic London weathering, soot and patina. No sky, no roof, no river, no ground, no perspective, no people, no shadows of other objects, no words, no borders. The whole image is only a mid-wall section and MUST repeat seamlessly left/right and top/bottom. Window bays physically each 4.25 metres wide, total facade tile 17 metres wide by 24 metres tall. Top row windows and bottom row windows equally aligned. Photographic neutral overcast lighting with soft ambient occlusion in the carved stone and window recesses, no strong directional lighting. Elegant historic masonry rather than brown brick. It must look like authentic architectural detail rather than an illustration or stylized model. Portrait-ish or square composition fitted edge to edge to the wall.
